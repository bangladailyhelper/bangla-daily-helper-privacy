# Privacy Policy – Bangla Daily Helper

**Last Updated:** November 19, 2026

## 1. Introduction

Welcome to Bangla Daily Helper. Your privacy is important to us. This Privacy Policy explains how the app handles information when you use its features.

## 2. Information Stored on Your Device

Bangla Daily Helper provides tools such as Calculator, Notes, Smart Reminder, Medicine Reminder, Expense Tracker, Due Book, Shopping List and other daily utilities.

Information you enter into Notes, Reminders, Expenses, Due Book and other locally stored features is saved on your own device.

We do not operate our own server to collect or store your personal notes, reminders, expenses or other locally saved information.

If you uninstall the app or clear its storage, locally saved information may be permanently deleted.

## 3. Advertising and Google AdMob

This app uses Google AdMob to display advertisements.

Google's advertising SDK may automatically collect or process certain information, including:

- IP address
- Advertising and device identifiers
- App interactions, such as ad views and taps
- Diagnostic and performance information

This information may be used for advertising, analytics, security and fraud prevention.

Google Privacy Policy:
https://policies.google.com/privacy

## 4. Currency Converter and Internet Usage

The Currency Converter feature uses the Frankfurter exchange-rate API to retrieve currency exchange-rate information.

The app may connect to the API when exchange-rate information is requested. We do not intentionally send your personal notes, reminders or financial records to this service.

Frankfurter API:
https://frankfurter.dev/

## 5. Google Play Purchases

If you purchase a Premium subscription or lifetime Premium feature, payment processing is handled through Google Play.

We do not directly collect or store your credit card number, debit card number or payment password.

Google Privacy Policy:
https://policies.google.com/privacy

## 6. Data Security

Information stored locally on your device is subject to your device's security settings.

We recommend keeping your device protected with a screen lock and not sharing sensitive information with others.

## 7. Children's Privacy

Bangla Daily Helper is a general-purpose utility application. We do not intentionally request children's personal information.

## 8. Third-Party Services

This app may use the following third-party services:

- Google AdMob
- Google Play Billing
- Frankfurter Exchange Rate API

These services may process information according to their respective privacy policies.

## 9. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy when app features, services or legal requirements change. The latest version will be made available through the Privacy Policy link.
